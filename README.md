# TravelMate – high-fidelity prototype

TravelMate is a budget-first leisure trip planner that builds flight + hotel packages and lets travelers compare them side by side. This is the interactive high-fidelity prototype for our CSC318 (University of Toronto) course project, Group Agent.

**Open the prototype:** https://kkkaa43.github.io/travelmate-prototype/

## How to use it
- Works in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). Nothing to install.
- On a computer it appears inside a phone frame. On a phone it fills the screen. For the best experience on a phone, open the link and use "Add to Home Screen".
- The scenario is pre-filled: Toronto → Tokyo, Mar 10–15 2027 (5 nights), 2 adults, CAD $3,000 budget. Tap **Find trip options** to start.
- Reloading the page, or **Profile → Reset prototype**, clears all choices so the next participant starts fresh.
- To try the plan-change flow: confirm a trip, open **My trips**, and tap **Study demo: simulate an airline schedule change**.

## Notes
- All prices, flights and hotels are sample data for usability testing. Nothing is booked or charged, and no data is stored or sent anywhere.
- Hotel photos are AI-generated images created by our team.
